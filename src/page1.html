To whoever finds this,

I do not know how many days it has been. There is no sun down here to count them by, only the lamp in the wall that never dims and never warms.

They call me a princess still. I can hear them saying it, far above, through the stone. Yet I don't feel treated as such, feeling confined to this hell-adjacent room.

The room is strange. It is a perfect square. There is nothing for myself except a table, a kot and a reinforced titanium door to remind me that escape is a foregone fantasy.

There is a sound in the walls like rain falling somewhere very far away, and it does not stop. It should be impossible, given the depth of this room in the earth.

If you are reading this, you are already deeper than anyone should be. Do not come further down to look for me. Whatever answers when you call my name in these caves, it will not be me.

Go back up.
Go back up while the stair is still behind you.

— A.
